Privacy

Delete your FocusPulse data

This page explains how to delete the data FocusPulse holds about you, what is removed, what is kept, and for how long. For the full policy, see our Privacy Policy.

Option 1: Delete data on your phone

Clear your sessions and settings

  1. Open FocusPulse.
  2. Tap Settings (the gear icon at the top right).
  3. Under DATA, tap Clear All Sessions and confirm.

This deletes your session history, focus scores and response times from your phone.

Remove everything from your phone

Uninstall FocusPulse from your phone (long-press the app icon and choose Uninstall, or use your phone's app settings). Uninstalling removes all data stored on your device, including your settings.

Anonymous analytics

FocusPulse sends anonymous usage data to Firebase (by Google). This includes app opens, feature use, session timing, feedback answers, any comment you choose to type on the feedback card, approximate country and city, and crash reports.

This data is not linked to your name, email address or phone number, and we have no accounts. Because it is anonymous, we cannot find or delete your individual records.

Event data is kept for 2 months. Analytics data tied to an anonymous app identifier is kept for up to 14 months. After those periods it is deleted automatically.

Request deletion by email

To ask us to delete anything you have sent us directly, such as an email from the Send Feedback option or a message to us, email us with the subject "Delete my data". Include the email address you wrote from so we can find your messages.

Email a deletion request

We respond within 48 hours and confirm once your messages are deleted.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted

  • Session history, focus scores and response times on your phone (Clear All Sessions, or uninstalling)
  • Your settings and preferences on your phone (uninstalling)
  • Messages you emailed to us, on request

Kept, and for how long

  • Anonymous event data: kept for 2 months, then deleted automatically
  • Analytics data tied to an anonymous app identifier: kept for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically
  • Anything we have not yet deleted from your request: kept only until the request is completed, which takes up to 48 hours

Questions

Email tryfocuspulse@gmail.com. We respond within 48 hours.
